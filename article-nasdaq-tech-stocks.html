Tech Stocks 2026: Nasdaq Records New High

February 20, 20267 min readBy Evans Mathibe

The Nasdaq Composite has shattered all previous records, climbing to unprecedented heights as artificial intelligence continues to transform the global economy. Tech giants lead the charge, but is there more room to grow?

The AI Investment Boom

Nvidia has become the most valuable company in the world, surpassing Apple and Microsoft with a market cap exceeding $4 trillion. The company's AI chips are in unprecedented demand, with supply unable to meet worldwide requirements.
