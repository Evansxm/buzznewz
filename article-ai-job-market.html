AI and the Future of Jobs

February 18, 20266 min readBy Evans Mathibe

As artificial intelligence continues to advance, the job market is undergoing a massive transformation. Here's what workers need to know about AI's impact on employment.

New Job Categories

AI is creating entirely new job categories including AI ethicists, machine learning engineers, data scientists, and AI trainers. These roles didn't exist a decade ago and are now among the highest-paying jobs in the tech sector.

Skills for the AI Era

Workers who understand AI tools and can collaborate with them are in highest demand. Critical thinking, creativity, and emotional intelligence remain distinctly human skills that complement AI capabilities.

The Path Forward

Experts recommend continuous learning and adaptability as key to thriving in an AI-driven economy.
